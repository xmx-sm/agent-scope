## 二、新增功能放哪里

| 新功能 | models | schemas | services | routers | 前端 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 对话/会话能力 | `models/agent/conversation.py` | `schemas/agent/chat.py` 或 `conversation.py` | `services/agent/conversation/` | `routers/agent/chat.py` | `Chat.vue` |
| 知识库 | `models/agent/knowledge.py` | `schemas/agent/knowledge.py` | `services/agent/knowledge/` | `routers/agent/knowledge.py` | `Knowledge.vue` |
| 超级智能体 / A2A | `models/agent/collaboration.py` | `schemas/agent/collaboration.py` | `services/agent/collaboration/` | `routers/agent/collaboration.py` | `SuperAgent.vue` / `A2A.vue` |
| 定时任务 | `models/agent/automation.py` | `schemas/agent/automation.py` | `services/agent/automation/` | `routers/agent/automation.py` | `Scheduled.vue` |
| Skills/MCP/子智能体 | `models/agent/resources.py` | `schemas/agent/resources.py` | `services/agent/resources/` | `routers/agent/resources.py` | 对应页面 |
| TTS/ASR 等媒体 | 按需 | `schemas/agent/media.py` | `services/agent/media/` | `routers/agent/media.py` | `Chat.vue` |
| 用户 Agent 配置 | `models/agent/config.py` | `schemas/agent/config.py` | `services/agent/config/` | `routers/agent/config.py` | `AgentSelfConfig.vue` |

## 三、约定

1. 路由层只解析参数与包装响应；业务在 `services/`。
2. 服务函数只接收一个 `params: dict`。
3. 同一领域的路由、服务、模型、Schema 使用同名子域，例如 `knowledge`。
4. 单个子域超过 3~5 个文件时继续拆文件，不把无关功能塞回 `__init__.py`。
5. 新增 Agent 子域时同步在 `routers/agent/__init__.py` 注册子路由。
6. 新表或新列：模型放 `models/agent/`，并在 `core/database.py` 补幂等 ALTER（存量库）。
7. 文档同步：`项目结构.md`、`CODE_INDEX.md`、`参数说明.md`、`接口说明.md`、`开发记录.md`。
